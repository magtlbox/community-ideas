# MagTlBox Community Ideas

Missing a tool, script, checklist, workbook, or other practical resource?

This is the place to suggest what might be useful.

## Before submitting

Search the existing **Discussions** first. Go to [Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/magtlbox/discussions/categories/ideas)

If someone has already described the same or a similar need, **upvote it instead of creating a duplicate**. Votes are one of the signals I use to see what people actually need and what may be worth building next.

If you can't find anything similar, create a new suggestion.

When describing your idea, focus primarily on:

* **What are you trying to do?**
* **What problem are you trying to solve?**
* **What is currently missing or unnecessarily difficult?**

You don't need to design the solution. A good description of the problem is often more useful than a feature list.

## What happens next?

Suggestions are not orders and do not guarantee implementation.

I use them to identify recurring needs and decide what may be worth exploring, prototyping, or adding to **MagTlBox**.

If an idea becomes a project, its development may continue in a dedicated repository or GitHub Issue.

Some resulting tools may become part of **MagTlBox Open**. Others may be released as maintained or paid resources.

## Suggest something

Go to **Discussions → Ideas** to:

1. search existing requests,
2. upvote something you need too,
3. or suggest something new.

**Need → signal → evaluate → build only when it makes sense.**
